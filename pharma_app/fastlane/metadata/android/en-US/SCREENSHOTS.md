# Play Store screenshots checklist

Use **phone** (16:9 or 9:16) and **7-inch tablet** assets as required by your Play listing. Export PNG or JPEG at Play’s current minimum resolutions.

Suggested captures (align with in-app labels):

1. **POS / checkout** — Active sale or cart with line items; shows retail workflow.
2. **Stock hub** — Balances or low-stock tab; communicates inventory control.
3. **Medicines** — Search or medicine detail; reinforces catalog strength.
4. **Purchases or receiving** — Draft or receive flow; appeals to owners/managers.
5. **Dashboard or home** — Authenticated landing that hints at breadth of modules.

Optional: **feature graphic** (1024×500) with product name, one tagline (“Pharmacy POS & stock for Ethiopia”), and brand colors matching the app (#2E7D32 / light green from web manifest theme).

Refresh screenshots after major UI releases so the listing matches the binary.
