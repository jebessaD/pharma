Pharmacy Management SaaS — V1 for Ethiopia
Goal

Build a simple pharmacy management SaaS for Ethiopian pharmacies focused on:

Sales + Inventory + Expiry + Multi-Branch + Team Management

Not a big hospital ERP.

Not online payment systems.

Just daily pharmacy operations made easy.

Very practical for the Ethiopian market.

Important Ethiopia Reality

Many pharmacies:

sell by piece, not full box
receive medicines without reliable barcode
use cash + bank transfer
manage stock manually
struggle with expiry tracking
need simple reports for inspections
have multiple pharmacists using one system

So your app must support this reality.

This is your competitive advantage.
